About Us

EverFlow Experience is a boutique ski school founded by Juan Rodrigo Fernandez — former Argentinian Ski Team racer, based in the Alps since 2022—together with his co-founder Gregorio Ubici, whose easygoing, people-first spirit turns every day on snow into a shared adventure.

After seasons teaching and riding across the U.S., Argentina, and the Alps, Juan built EverFlow to offer handpicked, bespoke ski days in the Bavarian Alps, grounded in expert coaching and authentic mountain culture.

Lessons are multilingual and fully tailored, blending Juan's race-honed technique with Gregorio's natural gift for connection and unforgettable hosting.

Meet Our Team

Juan Rodrigo Fernandez

Founder / Managing Director

Juan is a former member of the Argentinian Ski Team with over 60 FIS starts and more than 15 seasons racing at a high competitive level. Based in the Alps since 2022, he combines elite performance coaching with an engineering career in the energy sector, focused on decarbonization and sustainability.

At EverFlow, Juan leads the on-snow vision: precise technique, smart progression, and unforgettable days in the mountains.

Gregorio Ubici

Co-Founder / Operations Manager

Gregorio is an engineer and certified ski instructor who has dedicated his life to the mountains. Fueled by genuine passion, he spends back-to-back seasons between South America, Europe and United States.

At EverFlow, he designs and leads personalized ski experiences in English, Spanish, and Portuguese, turning each day on snow into a story people want to tell.

Jeremy Rossegger

Ski Instructor / Coach

Jeremy is a professional skier and certified Austrian Landeslehrer ski instructor with over four years of experience guiding and coaching skiers of all abilities. Raised in a family where skiing was a way of life, his passion for the mountains was inspired by his father, an experienced ski instructor who introduced him to the sport from an early age.

With Spanish roots and Australian, Argentinian, and Austrian heritage, Jeremy naturally connects with people from different cultures and creates a relaxed, welcoming atmosphere on and off the slopes. When winter ends, he follows his other lifelong passion for the sea, combining his work in skiing with the nautical world.

At EverFlow, Jeremy is known for his calm coaching style, strong technical foundation, and enthusiasm for helping every guest progress while enjoying every moment in the mountains.

Paolo Schwarz

Ski Instructor / Technique Specialist

Born and raised in Mallorca, Paolo found his passion for the mountains far from the island's beaches, first inspired by his father and quickly turned into a life on snow. In just four seasons he has earned the Staatlich geprüfter Skilehrer state certification through both the German Ski Instructors Association (DSLV) and the Austrian Ski Federation (ÖSV), a rare double credential for someone so young.

Confident and sharp-eyed, he is known for a quality of technical analysis you seldom find at his age. At EverFlow, Paolo turns that clarity into fast, visible progress for every guest, on any slope.

Felipe De La Fuente

Snowboard Instructor / Guide

Felipe has been sharing his passion for nature and mountain sports for over 20 years. As a fully certified snowboard instructor, he has worked across some of the world's most iconic mountain regions — from the East Coast, West Coast, and Rocky Mountains of the United States to Argentina and the European Alps.

His international experience, combined with a calm and intuitive teaching style, allows him to quickly understand each client's needs and help them progress with confidence. Whether working with beginners or advanced riders, Felipe focuses on creating a safe, enjoyable, and personalized learning environment.

Private, group, and family sessions are available, always tailored to each guest's level and expectations. More than just a lesson, Felipe creates complete mountain experiences — helping clients relax, explore, and fully enjoy the day.
