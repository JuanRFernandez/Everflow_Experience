EverFlow — Est. 2022

The days you remember are the ones built around you.

EverFlow Experience is a boutique alpine experience company founded by Juan Rodrigo Fernandez — former Argentinian Ski Team racer, based in the Alps since 2022. What began as a handpicked ski school in the Bavarian Alps has grown into something broader: complete, tailor-made alpine journeys.

From private coaching on snow to lodging, dining, wellness and seamless transport, every EverFlow itinerary is designed around one guest at a time — grounded in expert local knowledge and authentic mountain culture.

Experiences are multilingual and fully tailored, blending race-honed technique with the kind of hosting that turns a trip into a story people want to tell.

Meet Our Team

Juan Rodrigo Fernandez

Founder / Managing Director

Juan is a former member of the Argentinian Ski Team with over 60 FIS starts and more than 15 seasons racing at a high competitive level. Based in the Alps since 2022, he combines elite performance coaching with an engineering career in the energy sector, focused on decarbonization and sustainability.

At EverFlow, Juan leads the vision: precise technique on snow, smart progression, and complete alpine experiences — from lodging to logistics — crafted around each guest.

Our Guides & Instructors

Gregorio Ubici

Commercial Director / Ski School Operator

Gregorio is an engineer and certified ski instructor who has dedicated his life to the mountains, spending back-to-back seasons between South America, Europe and the United States. He runs the commercial side of the ski school and leads personalized experiences in English, Spanish, and Portuguese.

Jeremy Rossegger

Ski Instructor / Coach

A professional skier and certified Austrian Landeslehrer instructor with over four years guiding all abilities. Known for his calm coaching style and strong technical foundation, with Spanish roots and Australian, Argentinian, and Austrian heritage.

Paolo Schwarz

Ski Instructor / Technique Specialist

Born in Mallorca, Paolo holds the Staatlich geprüfter Skilehrer state certification through both the DSLV and ÖSV — a rare double credential. He turns sharp technical analysis into fast, visible progress for every guest, on any slope.

Felipe De La Fuente

Snowboard Instructor / Guide

Fully certified snowboard instructor with over 20 years across the world's most iconic mountain regions — from the U.S. coasts and Rockies to Argentina and the Alps. Calm, intuitive, and focused on complete mountain experiences.

Good To Know

Before we plan the first day.

Where do you operate?

We are headquartered in the Bavarian Alps and host across the region's finest destinations — Garmisch-Partenkirchen, Munich, Innsbruck, Kitzbühel, St. Moritz, Gstaad, Zermatt, Courchevel and Cortina d'Ampezzo — with trusted local partners in each.

What can an itinerary include?

Private coaching, lodging, dining, wellness, guiding, transfers and complete multi-day programs. Every element is optional — some guests book a single lesson, others a full week, handled A to Z.

Do I need a finished plan to enquire?

No. Start with whatever you know — a date, a group, an idea of the day — and we come back with the questions that matter and one clear proposal.

Which languages do you host in?

Experiences are hosted in English, Spanish and Portuguese, with German-speaking partners across the region.
